About Synapse

24H

One bridge. Two worlds. Endless possibilities.

Bridging academia
and industry.

The word Synapse represents a connection — a bridge between academia and industry. Synapse 2.0 is a 24-Hour National-Level Hackathon that brings together bright, driven minds to solve complex challenges through technology, creativity and collaboration.

In this fast-paced environment, participants work with peers, apply what they have learned and develop innovative solutions under pressure. It is more than a competition: it is a platform where classroom knowledge meets real-world expectations, and emerging talent gets noticed by the industry.

Our mission is to launch careers. By connecting promising students directly with technology companies, Synapse creates pathways from learning to opportunity. The best-performing students will have the opportunity to secure exclusive internship opportunities with our industry partners.

Need clarity?

Frequently asked
questions.

Who can participate?

Students can participate in teams of 2 or 4 members.

When and where is Synapse 2.0 happening?

The 24-hour hackathon will be held from 25 to 26 November 2026 at Jain College of Engineering, Belagavi.

What is the registration fee?

Early bird offer (valid until 16 November 2026): ₹600 for a team of 2 and ₹1,200 for a team of 4. After the early bird offer: ₹800 for a team of 2 and ₹1,600 for a team of 4. Registration closes on 24 November 2026.

What is included in the registration fee?

Breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, a welcome kit and a participation certificate are included for every participant.

What can participants win?

Synapse 2.0 features a total prize pool of ₹50,000 and 10+ company internship opportunities.

How do I register?

Use the Register your team button to open the official registration form. Complete the form before 24 November 2026.
